How it works

  1. Say it.Press a key anywhere on your Mac and say the thought. No window to switch to.
  2. It attaches.Trace notices the file, email or page in front of you and pins the note to it.
  3. It comes back.Reopen that thing next week and the note is waiting, with everything you left open.

It stays on your Mac

Capture, reading what is on screen and speech recognition all run on your Mac. Your notes live in a file you can open. Nothing is sent to a server to make Trace work.

Who it's for

  • Knowledge workersYou switch between ten apps a day and lose the thread of each.
  • ConsultantsMany clients, many decks. Pick each one up where you left it.
  • DevelopersLeave yourself the next step on the file, then let your coding agent read it.
